Closing Salvo

The classic fleet duel as a living 3D naval battle. Mark your targets, fold your iPhone Duo, and watch the salvo explode on the outer screen. Plays on every iPhone with iOS 17 or later.

Coming to the App Store

How to play

1 · Place your fleetDrag a ship; tap the selected ship to turn it. Ships can’t touch, not even at the corners. Shuffle places them for you.
2 · Pick targetsTap squares in enemy waters. Every ship you still have afloat fires one shell per round.
3 · FireOn iPhone Duo, fold the phone. On other iPhones, press the fire dial or swipe down.
4 · Read the waterA red square is a hit, a white cross is a miss. Sink the whole enemy fleet first to win.

Questions

Do I need an iPhone Duo?

No. Closing Salvo plays on every iPhone. On iPhone Duo, closing the phone fires the salvo and the cinematic plays on the outer screen; opening it brings the damage report. You can also keep playing on the outer screen while the phone is closed.

What is free, and what is Full Fleet?

Battles against the computer are free, with no ads. Your first two-player match is free too. Full Fleet is a one-time purchase that unlocks unlimited two-player matches, the Admiral (a tougher opponent) and the night and storm seas. There is no subscription.

How do I restore Full Fleet on a new iPhone?

On the title screen, tap Sea, Difficulty or Two players to open the Full Fleet screen, then tap Restore purchase. Use the same Apple Account you bought it with.

How does two-player mode keep fleets secret?

Each admiral places their ships in turn. Every time the phone changes hands, the screen is hidden first: on iPhone Duo the phone folds and the outer screen asks you to hand it over; on other iPhones a full-screen card covers the board until the next player is ready.

Is my battle saved?

Your settings are saved on your iPhone. A battle in progress is not kept if the app is closed from the app switcher; the next launch starts a fresh sea.

Sound

The music and sound effects can be switched off from the in-game menu (☰ › Sound) or with the speaker button during a battle.
