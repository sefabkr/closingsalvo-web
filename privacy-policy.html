Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 October 2026 · Applies to Closing Salvo (in Turkish: Amiral Battı) for iPhone

Short version: Closing Salvo collects no data. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and the game itself never connects to the internet. Purchases are handled entirely by Apple.

1. What we collect

Nothing. We do not collect, receive, store, share or sell any personal information or usage data. We have no servers that the app talks to. The app contains no third-party analytics, advertising, crash-reporting or tracking code, and it does not use the advertising identifier (IDFA).

2. What stays on your iPhone

To remember your choices, the app keeps a few small settings in its own storage on your device:

  • the difficulty and sea you picked, and whether you play the iPhone Duo or the classic controls;
  • whether you have seen the how-to card;
  • whether your free two-player match has been used, and how many battles you have won against the computer (used once, to suggest two-player mode).

These settings never leave your iPhone and are removed when you delete the app.

3. iPhone Duo hinge

On iPhone Duo the game reads whether the phone is open, half-open or closed, so that folding it can fire your salvo. This is handled on the device while you play and is never stored or sent anywhere.

4. Purchases

Full Fleet is an in-app purchase processed by Apple through the App Store. We never see your name, Apple Account or payment details. The app only learns from Apple whether Full Fleet is unlocked on your device. Apple’s handling of purchases is described in Apple’s own privacy policy.

5. Children

Because the app collects no information from anyone, it collects none from children either.

6. Changes

If the app ever changes what it does with information, we will update this page and the date above before that version is released.
